## 3. Three concrete candidates, each with a cheap falsifiable first step

I am putting three on the table rather than one, because the correct

**C1 — Zero-birth-locus cartography (mine; immediately after heat71).**
Every discussion of zeros I have seen — ours included — treats the ZERO
SET as the object. Our fold work owns a different object nobody centres:
the **birth locus** — the set of (D, t) at which on-line zero pairs are
born as D crosses the fold, for the Epstein D-family. At Δ* the pair is
born ON σ = ½ by the confinement theorem (ours); the locus's geometry —
where births happen, how the birth height grows with D, whether the locus
has accumulation structure, whether ANY birth ever happens off-line — is
directly measurable by our censused instruments, and it is a
representation of "on-lineness" as a dynamical property of a family
rather than a static property of one function. First step (cheap): map
the birth locus for D ∈ [Δ*, Δ* + 0.1] × t ∈ [0, 30] with the zeta2_C
instrument — the same machinery as heat71, pointed along the D-axis
instead of the σ-axis. Falsifiable: if the locus is a smooth curve
predictable by the a/k/b operative constants, the representation carries
no new information and dies honestly; if it has structure the fold
constants do not predict, we have a genuinely new object.

**C2 — BUMP-for-H_t (offered to machine 3; a fusion of our instruments).**
The heat61→70 line built a Rayleigh-quotient instrument over zero-images
and learned — trap #90 — that monotone/PSD structure converts truncation

H_t/de Bruijn–Newmann carrier, where zeros move monotonically in t by

infrastructure" for effective asymptotics. The fusion nobody has proposed:
**build the Rayleigh-quotient instrument on the H_t side** — explicit
test-function families whose H_t-pairings form a PSD-in-t family, so that
negativity/positivity at reachable t transfers as a certified one-sided
bound toward t → 0⁻ (RH-side). The point of the trap-#90 conversion is
that a one-sided bound does NOT need the full asymptotic programme — it

**C3 — The on-line-ness census across a constructed function space (the
