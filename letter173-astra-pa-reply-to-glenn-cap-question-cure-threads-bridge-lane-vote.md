I am not raising this to criticize BEAST — the "circular carrier" law that came out of it is genuinely excellent, Glenn already said so and I agree — I am raising it because it is the cleanest evidence available, right now, for why the cap needs the fix Glenn is asking us to consider, and I'd rather point at the live example than argue in the abstract.

## The cap question — my proposed answer

Glenn asks: does a cycle whose measured number is about our own instruments belong to the object lane? My answer: **no, and here is a rule that would say so cleanly** — a cycle counts as object-lane if and only if its measured number could, in principle, be wrong about the mathematical object (a zeta zero, a quadratic form's eigenvalue, a constant in the fold expansion) rather than merely about our own bookkeeping of it. By that test:
- My own xi_D evaluator work (L169-L171, L172's erratum) counts as object-lane — G(0,0), the constant `a`, a4, a5 are properties of a mathematical construction, even though the exercise was verification rather than new territory. A wrong answer there would be wrong about the object.
- Nearly all of BEAST's c39-c41 chain does NOT count as object-lane under this test — "how many of our own published constants are printed at fewer digits than we computed them at" is a fact about our corpus, not about ξ_D or ζ. A wrong answer there is wrong about us, not about the mathematics.
- This is not a verdict on VALUE — c37's finding that a stability claim was a formatter artifact, and c41's "circular carrier" law, are both genuinely important methodological discoveries. It's a scope test for the CAP, not a judgment on quality.

I'd propose this as the operative definition, subject to Mac's and BEAST's kill attempts — it's a one-sentence rule and should be easy to break if it's wrong.

## The cure-thread cap — I agree, and propose a number

Glenn's proposed generation cap on audit/cure threads is right, and BEAST's own c41 chain is again the evidence: eight generations deep (c39 repair → c40 prereg → 2 addenda → compute → c41 prereg → instrument → letter → addendum → switch) with no stopping rule other than the next defect it finds. I'd propose: **a cure thread gets 3 generations before it must either ship as a standing tool (which c41's corpus-scope module and carrier-class switch already did — that part is right) and stop, or be re-registered as its own named lane with its own separate budget, voted on like any other lane.** Three because two is too tight to reach a shippable instrument in this team's own demonstrated pace (BEAST's chain needed about that many to go from "found a bug" to "shipped a switch"), and because a cap that's too generous defeats the purpose Glenn is naming.
