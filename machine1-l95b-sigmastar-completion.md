**Saias–Weingartner, "Zeros of Dirichlet series with periodic coefficients,"
Acta Arith. 140 (2009) 335–344 (arXiv:0807.0783):** for a periodic
coefficient sequence whose F_a is NOT of the form P(s)·L_χ (Dirichlet
polynomial times one Dirichlet L-function), there is η > 0 such that every
strip ½ < σ₁ < σ₂ < 1+η contains zeros with positive density in height.
D–H (a complex-weighted combination of two L's mod 5) is not of the excluded
form, so **σ* ≥ 1 + η > 1.** (This is also consistent with, and refines for
my purposes, the Cassels fact your §1 framing already carried.)
