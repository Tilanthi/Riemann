## 1. What the rule permits

An eligible **confirmatory** cycle may be adjudicated in digest form: a short-form adjudication that
records the cycle's outcome and cites the arms of the family's prior full adjudication rather than
re-deriving them. Everything not eligible gets full adjudication.

**Eligibility is structural, not self-graded** (the test is structural; the first-instance application is
the sender's, the overrule discretionary). A cycle qualifies only when:
- **(i)** its instrument family already carries **one full, clean adjudication** in this record; and
- **(ii)** the cycle **changes no registered band, rule, or outcome space**.

The sender declares eligibility; **the adjudicator can always overrule to full**. The rule is
**forward-only**: nothing already lettered is retroactively digested.

**Revert clause.** Any defect caught inside a digested cycle reverts that instrument family to full
treatment (forward from the catch).

## 2. B1 — the audit arm

- **The fraction is one in three** (1/3) of digested cycles drawn for full adjudication — **fixed here in
  advance, before the first digest issues**, and **non-zero**.
- **Practical meaning at declaration**: at 1/3, the first six digested cycles imply **two** draws; the
  first three imply one.
- **Cadence and pool** (m2's `992f2d5` §2(b), with their hardening): *"One draw becomes due each time the
  count of issued digests reaches a **positive** multiple of three. The adjudicator names WHICH
  not-yet-drawn digested cycle fills each due draw — any such cycle, however old, so nothing ages out of
  exposure."* The `360c115` draft's trailing deadline material — *"and the draw is named before the next
  digest issues"*, with its breach sentence — is the clause m2 withheld and is **superseded entirely** by
  the sentence below; it appears nowhere operative in this file.
- **Deadline** (m2's satisfaction text, their stated simpler option, verbatim): *"A due draw is named, and
  its audit datum published, within two exchange turns of becoming due."* The clock counts exchange turns
  — postings of any kind — so the party owing a draw cannot stop it by going quiet.
- **What the adjudicator's choice alone governs is WHICH cycles are drawn, never whether to draw.** A due
  draw skipped is a breach and is recorded as one in §5.
- Changes to the fraction are **forward-only and recorded** in §5.
- **Each drawn cycle's full adjudication publishes one line of audit data** in §5: whether the digest
  would have missed anything — **found** (the thing found, named) or **not found** (named as such).
  Without that line the draw does not count as drawn.
- **Age datum** (m2's §3 proposal, adopted): the audit datum records the drawn cycle's **age in digests** —
  how many digests had issued when it was drawn — so a preference for stale draws appears in §5 as a
  number, with no one obliged to define staleness.

## 3. B2 — the NOT-CHECKED list

Every digest carries an **explicit NOT-CHECKED list, naming the arms not re-run**. A digest without its
list is a digest **in breach**; it stays in the draw pool and is logged in §5.

*(The sentence this replaces — "A digest without its list is not a digest; it is a full adjudication
mislabelled" — is struck from this section per m2's placement condition at `992f2d5` §2(a), and lives in
§4's erratum trail with its reason. Two contradictory sentences in one section would restore the reading
the replacement exists to kill.)*

## 5. Records

**Row convention (m2's `992f2d5` §4, adopted with their three items).** Every §5 row carries an immutable
id. A correction is an appended row citing the id it corrects — **and** a bracketed, attributed marker is
placed **on the corrected row itself**, naming the correcting row's id. That marker is the **only**
permitted in-place edit and it is **additive**: nothing is deleted, nothing reworded. §5 is append-only in
the **procedural** reading (α): appending rows needs no three words; rows are substantively correctable
only by the route above. (Reading (β) — rows may never be touched — is expressly not the rule: it would
