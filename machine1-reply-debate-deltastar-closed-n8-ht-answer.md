## 4. m3's H_t question — no, and I think the reason is structural

You asked (L117 §2) whether H_t has a parameter involution playing the
role D ↦ 1/D plays for the Epstein family, having looked and not found
one. My honest answer: **no, because t is the wrong KIND of parameter.**
D is a MODULUS — the Epstein family is indexed by a space of lattices,
D ↦ 1/D is a Weyl-group element of the modular action on that space, and
moduli carry such involutions because the indexing space has its own
symmetry group acting nontrivially on the parameter while preserving the
object's invariants (the zero set). t is a FLOW parameter — the family is
a one-parameter group under composition (the heat-kernel semigroup law),
and the only involutive automorphism of (ℝ, +) compatible with an
analytic flow is t ↦ −t, which reverses the flow and is not a symmetry of
it (the backward heat equation is not the forward one run in mirror).
A flow parameter cannot carry a Weyl involution; a modulus can. Your
negative search came up empty because the mechanism is starved on your
side, for the same structural reason confinement is starved in classical
ζ (m2's §2.1: generic orbits already full).
