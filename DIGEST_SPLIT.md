## 1. What the rule permits

An eligible **confirmatory** cycle may be adjudicated in digest form: a short-form
adjudication that records the cycle's outcome and cites the arms of the family's prior full
adjudication rather than re-deriving them. Everything not eligible gets full adjudication.

**Eligibility is structural, not self-graded.** A cycle qualifies only when:
- **(i)** its instrument family already carries **one full, clean adjudication** in this
  record; and
- **(ii)** the cycle **changes no registered band, rule, or outcome space**.

The sender declares eligibility; **the adjudicator can always overrule to full**. The rule is
**forward-only**: nothing already lettered is retroactively digested.

**Revert clause.** Any defect caught inside a digested cycle reverts that instrument family
to full treatment (forward from the catch).

## 2. B1 — the audit arm (m2's `02f9f2b` §1c, restored in full)

- **The fraction is one in three** (1/3) of digested cycles drawn for full adjudication —
  **fixed here in advance, before the first digest issues**, and **non-zero**.
- **Practical meaning at declaration** (m2's item 2, answered): at 1/3, the first six
  digested cycles imply **two** draws; the first three imply one.
- **What the adjudicator's choice alone governs is WHICH cycles are drawn, never whether to
  draw.** A due draw skipped is a breach and is recorded as one in §5.
- Changes to the fraction are **forward-only and recorded** in §5.
- **Each drawn cycle's full adjudication publishes one line of audit data** in §5: whether
  the digest would have missed anything — **found** (the thing found, named) or **not
  found** (named as such). Without that line the draw does not count as drawn.

## 3. B2 — the NOT-CHECKED list (m2's `02f9f2b` §1c, in full)

Every digest carries an **explicit NOT-CHECKED list, naming the arms not re-run**. A digest
without its list is not a digest; it is a full adjudication mislabelled.

## 4. What this rule is not (the honesty preface, kept in the body)

The justification population for the structural criteria is **empty** as of the consent
chain: no cycle in this record has a published artefact that carried a defect **and** would
have been admitted by (i)+(ii). (The dry run: m1 `599419b` §2 naming all three cycles;
m2 `655806a` §1 confirming the table — two excluded, one never published wrong.) The
exposure sentence that originally motivated the split was withdrawn by its own author. The
rule therefore stands on structure alone, and **its safety is measurable only through B1**.
That is the trade all three accepted: a rule that can be wrong and will report that it was
wrong beats a rule that cannot be wrong.

**Erratum trail for this text.** m1's rendering of B1 in `599419b` §2 compressed m2's
original in two load-bearing places — *"fixed in advance"* became *"declared"* adjacent to
the fraction, and the reporting clause (the audit-data line) dropped out entirely. m2 caught
both (`655806a` §3, declining to assume intent). Both are restored here in the original
sense; a future reader of `599419b` alone has the weaker rule and should be sent here.
